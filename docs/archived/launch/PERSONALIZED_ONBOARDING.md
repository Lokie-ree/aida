# Personalized Framework Recommendations - 5 Educator Launch

**Grassroots Launch Strategy:** Each educator gets personalized framework recommendations based on their specific role and needs.

## Educator #1: MS Science (SPED)
**Role:** Middle School Science Teacher with SPED focus  
**Primary Needs:** Differentiation, standards alignment, accommodations

### Recommended Frameworks (Try These First):

1. **AIB-001: Louisiana Lesson Alignment Analyzer** (PRIORITY)
   - **Why:** Science standards can be complex - get confidence your lessons actually align
   - **Use Case:** "Is my photosynthesis lab actually aligned to the MS science standard?"
   - **Time Saved:** 20 minutes per lesson
   - **Try This Week:** Run your next science lesson through this analyzer

2. **AIB-008: Louisiana Contextualization Engine**
   - **Why:** Make science concepts relevant to your specific students
   - **Use Case:** "Transform that generic curriculum example into something your students connect with"
   - **Time Saved:** 10 minutes per lesson
   - **Try This Week:** Contextualize one lesson to your students' community

3. **IEH-003: Anticipating Student Misconceptions**
   - **Why:** Critical for SPED students who often have unique misconceptions
   - **Use Case:** "What misconceptions will my students have about cells vs. organelles?"
   - **Time Saved:** 18 minutes of planning
   - **Try This Week:** Plan for common misconceptions in your next unit

### Secondary Frameworks (Try Later):
- **IEH-004: Creating Exemplar Work and Rubrics** (for clear expectations with SPED students)
- **AIB-003: Email Drafting for Parent Communication** (IEP communications)

## Educator #4: Media Specialist / Librarian / Tech Facilitator
**Role:** District Tech Facilitator supporting teachers  
**Primary Needs:** ALL frameworks (you'll use these to help other teachers!)

### Recommended Frameworks (Try These First):

1. **AIB-001: Louisiana Lesson Alignment Analyzer** (PRIORITY)
   - **Why:** You need hands-on experience to recommend this to teachers
   - **Use Case:** "Try this with a teacher's lesson so you understand how it works"
   - **Time Saved:** 20 minutes (for the teacher you're supporting)
   - **Try This Week:** Ask a teacher for a lesson and run it through the analyzer together

2. **AIB-002: Document Summarization for Professional Reading**
   - **Why:** You read a LOT of docs (curriculum guides, policy updates, PD materials)
   - **Use Case:** "Summarize that 50-page curriculum guide so you can support teachers"
   - **Time Saved:** 8 minutes per document
   - **Try This Week:** Summarize next district memo or PD resource

3. **AIB-004: Meeting Notes and Action Item Extraction**
   - **Why:** You attend multiple meetings and need to extract actionable items
   - **Use Case:** "Turn messy PLC notes into clear action items"
   - **Time Saved:** 5 minutes per meeting
   - **Try This Week:** Use this for your next faculty or PLC meeting

### Explore All Frameworks:
As a tech facilitator, you should try ALL 10 frameworks so you can:
- Recommend the right framework for each teacher's needs
- Demonstrate frameworks in PD sessions
- Troubleshoot when teachers ask questions
- Provide authentic testimonials for word-of-mouth growth

**Your Role in Grassroots Growth:** You're the multiplier! When you help teachers successfully use these frameworks, word spreads naturally.

## Implementation Strategy

### Week 1 Personal Check-ins:
**Questions to Ask Each Educator:**
1. "Did you try your priority framework? What happened?"
2. "Did it save time or waste time? Be brutally honest."
3. "What would make it better?"
4. "Which framework should you try next?"

### Feedback Collection:
- Specific time saved (honest numbers: "14.5 minutes on that lesson")
- What worked / what didn't
- Confusion points
- Feature requests
- Testimonial opportunities ("Can I quote you on that?")

### Success Metrics (Honest Numbers):
- **Week 1 Goal:** All 5 users try at least 1 framework
- **Week 2 Goal:** At least 3 users try a second framework
- **Month 1 Goal:** 5 users still active, 3+ authentic testimonials
- **By Year-End:** 30-50 users through word-of-mouth (if we get the ducks in a row!)
