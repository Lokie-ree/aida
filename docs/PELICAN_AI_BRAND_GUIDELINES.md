## 🎯 Brand Identity

### **Brand Name**
**Pelican AI** - Named after Louisiana's state bird, the Brown Pelican, symbolizing guidance, protection, and Louisiana pride.

### **Tagline**
**"Your AI Guidance Partner"** - Emphasizes partnership, guidance, and support rather than just tools.

### **Mission Statement**
We're Louisiana educators who understand the challenges teachers face. We're tired of waiting for LDOE to give us guidance on AI tools. So we're building it ourselves—Louisiana educators, for Louisiana educators.

Pelican AI is the "colleague you wish you had"—the master teacher who checks your work and gives you confidence. It's not another AI tool to learn. It's guidance that works with whatever AI tool you already use.

### **Founder Story**
Pelican AI was built by a Louisiana educator who understands the challenges teachers face. The founder was "that teacher—grading papers at 11 PM, drowning in admin work, anxious about using AI tools because 'what if I get it wrong?'"

When ChatGPT launched, the need became clear: Louisiana educators needed someone to tell them "Here's how to use this responsibly. Here's what aligns with Louisiana standards. Here's what won't get you in trouble."

That person didn't exist. So Pelican AI became that person—the colleague you wish you had, the "master teacher" who checks your work and gives you confidence.

**Founder Credentials:**
- 15+ years Louisiana classroom experience
- Technology facilitator, Jefferson Parish
- Built Pelican AI in 52 days while teaching full-time
- Platform-agnostic approach (works with ANY AI tool)

## 📧 Email Templates

### **Beta Launch Email Sequence**

#### **Day 1 Morning - Welcome**
- **Subject**: "You're in! Start with this framework (5 minutes)"
- **Tone**: Casual, encouraging, action-oriented
- **Key Elements**:
  - Direct login link
  - 5-minute quick start checklist
  - Specific first framework to try ("Lesson Objective Unpacker")
  - Request for immediate feedback ("Did this save you time? Yes/No?")
- **Signature**: "— Randall" (personal, not corporate)
- **P.S.**: "I'm around all day if you get stuck. Text me: [your number]"

#### **Day 1 Evening - Check-In**
- **Subject**: "Quick check-in: Did you get in?"
- **Tone**: Supportive, problem-solving
- **Purpose**: Identify blockers (technical, confusion, time)
- **Approach**: Direct questions, offer help

#### **Day 3 - Feedback Request**
- **Subject**: "Real talk: Is this useful?"
- **Tone**: Honest, direct, feedback-seeking
- **Key Message**: "Brutally honest is helpful. 'This is confusing' is better than silence."
- **Questions**: Have you used frameworks? What worked? What didn't? What stopped you?

#### **Day 7 - Testimonial Request (Conditional)**
- **Subject**: "Can I quote you?"
- **Tone**: Appreciative, permission-seeking
- **Conditional Logic**: 
  - If positive feedback → Request testimonial with exact quote
  - If no engagement → Check-in: "I haven't heard from you—is everything okay?"
- **Approach**: Show exact quote, ask permission, offer edits

### **Tech Facilitator Email Sequence**

#### **Post-Meeting Thank You**
- **Subject**: "Thanks for committing to the Pelican AI pilot!"
- **Tone**: Grateful, organized, supportive
- **Content**: Pilot timeline, success criteria, next steps

#### **Weekly Check-Ins**
- **Week 1**: Status update, identify blockers, schedule call
- **Week 2**: Celebrate wins, request testimonials
- **Week 3**: Testimonial collection, principal meeting prep
- **Week 4**: Pilot complete, scale discussion

### **Newsletter**
- **Subject**: "Pelican AI Weekly - [Topic]"
- **Format**: Clean, scannable, actionable
- **Content**: Tips, updates, community highlights, educator success stories

### **Email Tone Principles**
- **Be Human**: Use first name, casual tone, personal signature
- **Be Direct**: Clear asks, specific questions, actionable CTAs
- **Be Honest**: Acknowledge early-stage, ask for real feedback
- **Be Supportive**: Offer help, check in, solve problems

- ❌ "AI tools for teachers"

- **Product Aware**: "Tired of learning new AI tools?"

#### **Early-Stage Messaging (Current)**
- "Starting with 3 colleagues. Your feedback literally shapes everything."
- "3 educators testing. Building together."
- "Built by Louisiana educators, for Louisiana educators"

- ✅ **DO**: Use honest numbers ("3 colleagues" not "500+", not even "10" when you have 3)

## 🚫 Brand Don'ts

### **Avoid**
- Generic AI tool language
- Corporate jargon or buzzwords ("leverage synergies", "paradigm-shifting")
- Overly technical explanations
- Promises of replacing human judgment
- Claims about specific AI platforms only
- Fake social proof ("500+ educators" when you have 3, "10 educators" when you have 3)
- Placeholder testimonials ("[Your Name Here]")
- Over-promising ("Save 10 hours per week!" when unrealistic)
- Generic value props ("Perfect for all subjects!")

### **Never**
- Position as "the only AI solution"
- Ignore Louisiana context
- Overwhelm with technical details
- Promise unrealistic time savings
- Compete with educator expertise
- Use fake numbers or testimonials
- Ignore non-users (always follow up)
- Build features users don't want before validating need

### **Common Pitfalls to Avoid**

1. **Fake Social Proof**
   - ❌ "500+ educators" when you have 3
   - ❌ "10 educators" when you have 3
   - ✅ "Starting with 3 colleagues. Building together."

2. **Over-Promising**
   - ❌ "Save 10 hours per week!" (unrealistic)
   - ✅ "Save 14.5 minutes per lesson" (specific, validated)

3. **Corporate Jargon**
   - ❌ "Leverage AI synergies"
   - ✅ "Stop grading papers at 11 PM"

4. **Ignoring Non-Users**
   - ❌ Only focusing on engaged users
   - ✅ "What stopped you from logging in?" (follow up)

5. **Building Unvalidated Features**
   - ❌ Complex features when simple ones aren't used yet
   - ✅ Build what users actually need (Framework Library improvements)

## 🎯 Marketing Principles (Cialdini's 6 Principles)

### **1. Social Proof** (Most Powerful)
- ✅ **DO**: "Starting with 3 colleagues", "3 educators testing", real testimonials with names and schools
- ❌ **DON'T**: Fake numbers or "[Your Name Here]" placeholders
- **Application**: Use honest early-stage numbers. Real quotes > fake scale. Starting small is authentic.

### **2. Reciprocity** (Give Before You Get)
- ✅ **DO**: Free frameworks, immediate value, no paywall for core features
- ❌ **DON'T**: Paywall everything, require signup for basic guidance
- **Application**: Framework Library accessible without login. Beta is 100% free.

### **3. Scarcity** (Create Urgency)
- ✅ **DO**: "Starting with 3 colleagues", "Your feedback literally shapes everything"
- ❌ **DON'T**: Fake scarcity ("Only 3 spots left!" when there's no limit, "First 100 educators only" when you have 3)
- **Application**: Authentic grassroots approach. Early members literally shape everything. No fake urgency.

### **4. Authority** (Build Credibility)
- ✅ **DO**: "Built by Louisiana educator with 15+ years experience", founder credentials
- ❌ **DON'T**: Generic claims without proof
- **Application**: Founder story, specific credentials, Louisiana-specific knowledge.

### **5. Consistency** (Get Small Commits)
- ✅ **DO**: Onboarding checklist, "first win in 5 minutes", small steps
- ❌ **DON'T**: Overwhelm with 20 features at once
- **Application**: Day 1 email asks for one framework try. Build momentum gradually.

### **6. Liking** (Be Human)
- ✅ **DO**: Founder story, casual tone, teacher-to-teacher, personal signature
- ❌ **DON'T**: Corporate jargon, overly formal, faceless brand
- **Application**: "— Randall" signature, personal check-ins, authentic communication.
