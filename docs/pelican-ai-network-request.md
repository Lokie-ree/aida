## Overview

I am following up on my previous request to allow Pelican AI through our district network filters. The platform is currently being blocked by Content Keeper (CK), which is preventing multiple teachers from accessing this professional development tool during their work hours.

## What is Pelican AI?

Pelican AI is an intelligent coaching assistant designed specifically for Louisiana K-12 educators. It helps teachers generate high-quality, Louisiana-aligned prompts for use in AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and MagicSchool AI.

**Important:** Pelican AI is a **teacher-facing professional development tool only**. It is **NOT used with students** at any time.

## Teacher Use Cases

Pelican AI supports educators in the following professional activities:

### 1. **Lesson Planning**
- Generating prompts to create Louisiana Student Standards-aligned lesson plans
- Developing instructional materials that align with the Louisiana Educator Rubric (LER)
- Creating grade-level appropriate content for classroom instruction

### 2. **Assessment Development**
- Creating assessment prompts aligned with Louisiana standards
- Developing rubrics that match LER indicators
- Generating formative and summative assessment materials

### 3. **Parent Communication**
- Drafting professional communication templates
- Creating clear, standards-aligned explanations of student learning objectives
- Developing parent-friendly resources about curriculum and expectations

### 4. **IEP Accommodations**
- Generating prompts to develop appropriate accommodations
- Creating individualized support materials aligned with Louisiana standards
- Supporting special education planning and documentation

### 5. **Professional Development & Evaluation Preparation**
- Creating prompts that help teachers prepare for LEADS evaluations
- Generating materials that demonstrate LER indicator alignment
- Supporting professional growth and reflection activities

### 6. **Data Analysis & Planning**
- Developing prompts for analyzing student data
- Creating planning templates for instructional improvement
- Supporting evidence-based instructional decision-making

## Key Platform Characteristics

- **Platform-Agnostic:** Pelican AI generates prompts that teachers copy/paste into their preferred AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, etc.). It does not replace these tools.
- **Louisiana-Specific:** Built specifically for Louisiana educators, with knowledge of Louisiana Student Standards, the Louisiana Educator Rubric, and LEADS evaluation framework.
- **Teacher-to-Teacher Design:** Created by a Louisiana educator for Louisiana educators, focusing on authentic, practical support.

## Student Safety & Usage Policy

**Pelican AI is NOT used with students:**

- ❌ No student-facing features
- ❌ No student data collection

**Pelican AI is ONLY used by teachers:**
